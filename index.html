Dad Basics Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Dad Basics ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Dad Basics operates entirely offline on your device. No information is transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information such as:

  • Child's age (for age-appropriate lesson recommendations)
  • Checklist preferences and progress
  • Lesson completion status

All such information is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted anywhere.

AI Features (BYOK)

Our AI Ask-Anything feature uses a "Bring Your Own Key" model:

  • You provide your own API key from a provider of your choice (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, etc.)
  • Your API key is stored locally on your device
  • AI queries are sent directly to your chosen provider — we never see or store your prompts or responses
  • We do not have access to your AI conversations

Third-Party Services

Dad Basics may use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services — for app updates and crash reporting (optional)
  • Your chosen AI provider — if you use BYOK features, your queries go directly to them

Data Security

Since we collect no data, there is nothing for us to lose or have stolen. Your local data stays on your device. If you uninstall the app, all local data is deleted.

Your Rights

  • Access: View your data in the app at any time
  • Deletion: Uninstall the app to delete all local data
  • Export: Export your data via the app's settings (coming soon)

Children's Privacy

Dad Basics is designed for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is not directed to children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
